# Product: Alafia (working title)

## One-line pitch
A wellness companion that turns daily habits into a game character. Log movement, meals and rest in seconds, your character levels up, and a short coach line reacts. Local-first, so it works with patchy data.

## Who it is for
Young adults in Nigeria and similar data-conscious, low-connectivity settings who want a light way to build healthy routines without a heavy tracker.

## The one core loop (everything serves this)
1. Log: Move, Eat or Rest in under 10 seconds.
2. Stats update: a pure, deterministic engine awards XP and raises stats.
3. Coach reacts: one or two encouraging sentences.

If a feature does not strengthen this loop, it is out of scope.

## Hard rules
- No medical advice, diagnosis, dosing, calorie targets or weight targets. The coach encourages and reflects only.
- Zero-judgment tone. Never shame missed days, short sleep or food choices.
- Health data stays on the device by default. No accounts in the MVP.
- Anything sent to an AI provider is anonymous aggregate data only: event type, XP gained, level, streak, top stat, time bucket, tone. Never names, free text or emails.
- The app must stay fully usable with no network and with every AI provider failing.
- User-facing text is plain English, short sentences, with Nigerian food examples where food is shown.

## Non-goals for the MVP
Accounts, social features, wearable integrations, calorie counting, payments, push notifications, on-device language models.

## What "done" looks like for the hackathon
A judge opens the live link, logs three things in under a minute, sees the character level up and the coach react, then switches to airplane mode and keeps logging.

## Voice
Warm, short, a little playful. Game words (quest, level, XP) are fine. Avoid fitness-culture pressure words such as "burn", "shred", "cheat meal".
